Exploration FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our curation standards, tasting passes, and bakery discovery guidelines.

Are the recipes and techniques in the journal verified by professional bakers?

Yes! Every article, hydration formula, and lamination guide is written or reviewed by certified pastry chefs and master sourdough bakers.

How frequently are new bakery guides and articles published?

We publish twice weekly, highlighting in-depth baker interviews on Tuesdays and culinary science and technique guides on Fridays.

Can I submit my own bakery story or guest article?

We welcome submissions from practicing bakers, chocolatiers, and culinary historians. Send our editorial team a message through our Contact page.

Do you offer sensory tasting kits accompanying the articles?

Yes! Our Connoisseur Guild members receive quarterly tasting boxes featuring the specific bean-to-bar chocolates and specialty confections highlighted in our articles.
